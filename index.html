HRM-Fin

A pre-registered experiment that did not win.

Does HRM-style recursion, a fast module nested inside a slow one, predict next-day open-to-close direction for the 17 TOPIX-17 ETFs from US sector ETFs? I froze the statistics, the data matrix and the sealed test folds before training anything, then matched both models at exactly 50,019,730 FLOPs.

The N4 ensemble finished at 0.5548 out-of-fold LogLoss, short of the 0.5451 classical floor. Against its matched baseline SSR-6, the pre-registered rules return “indeterminate”. I report it exactly like that.
